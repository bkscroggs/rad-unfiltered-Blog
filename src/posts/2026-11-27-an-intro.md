If you spend enough time behind the control console of a CT scanner, you learn quickly that there is often a gap between glossy vendor press releases and what actually happens on the hospital floor at 2:00 AM. My name is Bryan Scroggs, and after 20 years working as a hospital CT Technologist, I created **Radiology Unfiltered** to bridge that gap.

> "New technology only matters if it works safely, reliably, and efficiently in clinical practice—not just on an exhibit hall floor."
> <cite>Radiology Unfiltered Editorial Mission</cite>

## Why Radiology Unfiltered?

Medical imaging moves fast, and keeping up with the constant stream of regulatory clearances, hardware breakthroughs, and clinical studies can be overwhelming. **Radiology Unfiltered** is built as a dedicated space to track, break down, and discuss the real-world developments shaping modern radiology without the marketing spin.

## What You Can Expect from This Blog

### Cross-Modality News and Innovations

While my clinical roots are deep in CT, modern diagnostic imaging is an interconnected ecosystem. Coverage spans major updates across **CT, MRI, X-ray, Mammography, Ultrasound, Nuclear Medicine, Interventional Radiology, and Radiology AI**.

### Practical, Floor-Level Context

Posts look beyond vendor headlines to examine how new scanners, reconstruction algorithms, and clinical protocols actually affect **image quality, patient radiation dose, room throughput, and technologist workflows**.

### Evidence-Backed Reporting

Every article is grounded in verified clinical data, peer-reviewed literature, FDA clearances, and primary industry announcements—complete with direct links to the original sources so you can inspect the research yourself.

## Join the Conversation

Whether you are a fellow technologist, a radiologist, a student preparing for your registry, or simply someone fascinated by the science of looking inside the human body, welcome. My goal is to keep the analysis candid, clinically accurate, and free of unnecessary fluff.
